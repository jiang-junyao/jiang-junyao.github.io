Main research topic

From DeepTrack barcoding to transferable fate maps.

  • Cell fate decisions explain how pluripotent cells develop into diverse tissues, and how disruptions can lead to congenital abnormalities.
  • In vivo single-cell multi-omics lineage tracing has enabled early lineage priming discovery in mouse embryos and fate-resolved GRN reconstruction.
  • Because direct genetic barcoding is ethically and technically infeasible in human embryos, transfer learning can provide non-invasive in silico fate mapping from 3D human embryo data.
