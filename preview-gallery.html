I love snow mountains, plateaus, and grasslands. After staring at screens, office buildings, and deadlines for too long, the best part of being out there is simple: mountains don’t chase me for drafts, grasslands don’t schedule meetings, and snow peaks never ask for progress updates.

我喜欢雪山、高原和草原。平时看多了屏幕、写字楼和 deadline，偶尔跑到这些开阔的地方，最大的好处就是：山不会催我交稿，草原不会拉我开会，雪山也不会问我进度。
